## Curtailment release rules

Total curtailment 2025–2026: **3,858,406 MWh**. Share at negative price (economic, releasable): **86.4%** (pooled 84.7%). The remainder is network-bound — congestion and system strength — and does not go away when demand rises.

### Marginal release rate (MW released per MW of net-demand rise)

| horizon | all steps | curtailment active | > 200 MW | price < 0 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 5 min | -0.349 | -0.507 | -0.626 | -0.671 |
| 30 min | -0.266 | -0.407 | -0.548 | -0.623 |
| 1 hour | -0.245 | -0.366 | -0.498 | -0.563 |
| 3 hours | -0.241 | -0.331 | -0.421 | -0.464 |

### The rule

For a counterfactual raising net demand by Δ at a step with curtailment C and price p:

```
released  =  min( C,  k · Δ )

k = 0.42   if C > 200 MW and p < 0
k = 0.33   if C >  10 MW
k = 0.24   otherwise

retained  >=  13.622 % of C      the network-bound floor
```

(pooled 2022–2026 gave 0.41 / 0.31 / 0.20 and a 15.3% floor.)
